# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want 3d-puc to be a welcoming project for everyone who wants to help, whatever their age, background, identity, experience level, nationality, religion, appearance or ability. Students, alumni, staff and people who have never set foot on campus are all welcome here.

## Our standards

Things that help build a good community:

- Being kind and patient, especially with newcomers and first-time contributors.
- Respecting different viewpoints, languages and levels of experience. Portuguese and English are both fine in issues and PRs.
- Giving feedback on the work, not the person, and taking feedback gracefully.
- Owning mistakes, apologizing, and learning from them.
- Putting the project and its users first: students and visitors trying to find a building.

Things that are not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Threats, intimidation, stalking, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as address, email or photos, without their permission.
- Trolling, or spamming issues and PRs.
- Anything else that would be considered inappropriate in a professional or university setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other channel used for the project. It also applies when someone represents the project in public, for example as a PUC Tech member presenting it.

## Responsibilities of maintainers

Maintainers are responsible for explaining these standards and for acting when they are not met. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for behavior they judge inappropriate, threatening or harmful. They will explain the reason when they can do so safely.

## Reporting

If you see or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it to the maintainers. You can reach them through the [PUC Tech site](https://puctech.com.br), or by contacting a maintainer directly on GitHub (see the repository's [contributors](https://github.com/PUCTech/3D-puc/graphs/contributors)). Please don't report in a public issue if it would expose you or someone else.

All reports are handled promptly and in confidence. Maintainers who are involved in an incident will step aside from deciding it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will usually follow these steps, but may skip ahead if the behavior is serious:

1. **Correction.** A private or public note explaining what was inappropriate and why.
2. **Warning.** A formal warning, with consequences if the behavior continues.
3. **Temporary ban.** A time-limited ban from project spaces.
4. **Permanent ban.** For repeated or severe violations, including harassment.
